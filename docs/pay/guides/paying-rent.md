Lendiom Pay is the online portal the company you pay uses to collect rent. This page covers what a
rental looks like, what the amount you are asked for is made of, how to pay it, and where rent works
differently from a loan payment. "The company you pay" means the company you rent from — they set up
your account and control the settings described here.

## What you see for a rental

Your rentals sit under **Rentals** on the home screen. That section only appears when you have at
least one rental. Each row shows the name, the status underneath, and the amount owed on the right.
The amount is red when money is owed, and the dollar icon turns red when the status is **Late**.

![The Lendiom Pay home screen at phone width, showing the tenant’s rental](/img/docs/pay/guides/paying-rent/01.png)

Tap **View Details**. The page opens with the rental name and **Balance Due**, and the help icon
beside that amount says the balance due is a total of the unpaid payments and any late fees. Four
cards sit below.

| Card | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Next Due Date** | The next date rent is due on this rental |
| **Last Payment Date** | The most recent payment received. A dash means none yet |
| **Status** | Current, Late, Eviction, Evicted, or Terminated |
| **Payment Amount** | Your regular rent |

![A rental open in Lendiom Pay at phone width, showing the rental name and balance due](/img/docs/pay/guides/paying-rent/02.png)

Under the cards is your history: the three most recent entries, with **Load more** when there are
more. An entry can be a Regular Payment, a Late Fee, a Deposit, or a Maintenance, Documentation,
Early Termination, or Other Fee. Late fee rows are red.

Tap an entry for its details — the total, how much went to your balance, how much went to fees, the
platform fee, the method, and the status. On a **Late Fee**, read **Amount to Fees**: that line is the
size of the fee. The amount on the row is what was collected from you at the time, so a fee charged
to your balance rather than collected shows there as ($0.00).

## What the amount includes

A rental's **Balance Due** is two things added together:

- **Rent that has come due and has not been paid.** This is rebuilt every night: one rent payment for
  every due date reached since your first payment date, minus everything applied to rent. Paying
  ahead is not lost — that money stays counted, so the balance does not creep back.
- **Your late fee balance.**

Nothing else is in it. Deposits, maintenance fees, documentation fees, early termination fees, other
fees, and recurring fees show in your history when they are charged, but they are not part of Balance
Due and no button in the portal pays them. If you have been told you owe one, ask the company you pay
how they want it handled.

:::caution The rental and the total at the top can disagree
The amount shown on the rental — on its row and on its own page — includes your late fees. The big
**Balance Due** at the top of the home screen counts only the rent on that rental, without the late
fees. So when you have late fees, the rental shows more than it added to the total and the rows will
not add up. Pay from the rental's own amount. See
[Your Lendiom Pay Home Screen](../home-screen.md).
:::

:::info The two date cards at the top are not about your rental
**Next Due Date** at the top of the home screen is built from loans and invoices only, and **Last
Payment Date** from loans only. Neither one looks at rentals, so if a rental is all you have, both
show a dash even though your rental has real dates. Open the rental to see them.
:::

## Paying your rent

Open the rental and tap **Make a Payment**. You need a saved card or bank account first — see
[Adding a Payment Method](./adding-a-payment-method.md).

Pick the method at the top of the payment page. Your default is chosen for you, anything marked
**(disabled)** cannot be used, and the **+** button goes to the Payment Methods page. Then choose the
amount.

| Choice | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance Due** | Unpaid rent plus late fees. Selected when the page opens |
| **Regular Payment** | Your rent amount. Hidden if it falls below a minimum set on your rental |
| **Payment Amount** | A minimum set by the company you pay. Only appears when it differs from the other choices |
| **Custom Amount** | You type the figure. Prefilled with the minimum, if there is one |

If your rental is in **Eviction**, the other choices are hidden and only **Balance Due** is offered.
The page says so: since the rental is in eviction mode, you must pay the total balance due.

When there is a minimum it is printed under the choices, and a custom amount below it turns the box
red and keeps **Pay** switched off until you raise it. Type amounts as plain digits, like `1200.00`.

![The rental payment page in Lendiom Pay at phone width, with the payment method and amount](/img/docs/pay/guides/paying-rent/03.png)

**Total Amount** is what will actually leave your account. Depending on how the company you pay set
things up, it may add a processing fee, half of one, or none; the note under the field says which.

Tap **Pay**, then **Authorize**. Nothing is charged until you authorize. The notice always says the
money will be applied as a regular payment — that is its wording even when part of your payment
clears late fees first. Where the money landed is recorded on the entry afterward.

![The Transaction Details window for a rent payment in Lendiom Pay, showing the total split between the balance and fees](/img/docs/pay/guides/paying-rent/04.png)

A green message confirms the payment, your balance drops right away, and a new entry appears with
**Status: Pending**. Pending is normal — the processor has not confirmed the funds settled yet.
[Making a Payment](./making-a-payment.md) covers pending, failed payments, and receipts in full.

## How rent differs from a loan payment

| | On a rental | On a loan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No payment method saved** | No buttons appear at all | The header shows **Add Payment Method** |
| **Paying extra** | Allowed; it counts toward future rent | Cannot exceed the total owed on the loan |
| **Where extra goes** | Not offered | **How to Apply Extra**: Principal or Next Payment |
| **Principal-only payments** | Not applicable | Offered once the balance is $0.00 |
| **Balance breakdown** | Not available | Available where interest builds daily |
| **Fees charged separately** | No way to pay them in the portal | Other fees, down payments and setup fees each have a button |
| **Automatic payments** | **Enable Auto Pay**, set a maximum, no Edit | **Enable Auto Draft**, set a fixed amount, Edit available |
| **While marked Late** | You can still pay and still turn on Auto Pay | Auto Draft is hidden until the loan is current |

See [Setting Up Automatic Payments](./automatic-payments.md). On a rental you set a maximum rather
than a fixed amount, and each month only what you actually owe is taken, up to that maximum.

## When the buttons are missing

:::warning With no saved payment method, a rental shows no button at all
On a loan the header offers **Add Payment Method**. On a rental it offers nothing — no **Make a
Payment**, no **Enable Auto Pay** — so there is nothing to tap and no hint about what is wrong. This
is not something you did. Open the menu, go to **Payment Methods**, add a card or bank account, then
return to the rental and the buttons will be there.
:::

<!-- screenshot: at phone width, a rental details page for someone with no saved payment method — the header shows Add a Payment Method rather than Pay Rent -->

Two other reasons the buttons may be gone:

- **Online payments are off for this rental.** The payment page shows "Online payments are not
  enabled for this rental" and returns you to the rental. Ask the company you pay how to pay instead.
- **The lease has ended.** A Terminated or Evicted rental cannot be paid here and drops off your home
  screen. Nothing was deleted and your history is not lost. If you are still expected to pay, contact
  the company you pay.

If the payment page fails to open you will see "We couldn't load this payment page" with **Try Again**
and **Back to Rental**. Try again first; a weak connection is the usual cause. For red messages with a
number in them, see [Error Messages in Lendiom Pay](./error-messages.md).

:::tip Who to ask
Your rent amount, due dates, late fees, and whether online payments are on all belong to the company
you pay. Lendiom Support cannot change any of them.
:::
