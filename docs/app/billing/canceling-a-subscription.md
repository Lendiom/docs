title: Canceling a Lendiom Subscription
---

**Introduction**

Organization admins can cancel their Lendiom subscription directly from the billing settings.

This process can be used when an organization wants to stop its active subscription or end future billing.

**Cancelling a Subscription**

To cancel a Lendiom subscription:

1. Log in to **Lendiom**.
2. Go to **Org Settings**.
3. Select **Billing**.
4. Click **Cancel** next to **Action**.
5. Follow the confirmation prompts to complete the cancellation.

Once the cancellation is confirmed, the subscription cancellation will be processed according to the billing terms shown during the confirmation flow.
